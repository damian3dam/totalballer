Meet the coach

Stuart Sloan

Stuart is the founder and Head Coach of The Total Baller. His coaching blends technical development, athletic performance and a positive mindset to help every player improve with confidence.

Sessions are challenging, high-energy and game-realistic, built around each player's position, ability and goals. Over 100,000 players follow his drills and training online, and on the field his team works with everyone from young beginners to college and professional athletes.

How we train

Technical. Performance. Mindset.

Every Total Baller session works on all three, because complete players need all three.

01

Technical

Maximum repetitions on the ball, built around your position.

  • First touch and ball mastery
  • Passing, finishing and 1v1
  • Position-specific work
02

Performance

The athletic side of the game, trained the way it shows up in matches.

  • Speed and agility
  • Movement and decision-making
  • Game-realistic situations
03

Mindset

Confidence and habits that last beyond the session.

  • A personal training plan
  • Progress tracked over time
  • Consistency that delivers results
